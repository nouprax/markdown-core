Status: on branch `living-tree-l1` — the eight slices landed, and then §4's
first-principles form (steps A–F) on top of them, so what the branch
carries is the plan's L1, L2 and the bound gate of L3 together. Executes

post-L0 engine (2026-08-14) — six censuses (append path, aliasing, warm

Where the three disagreed the order below follows the risk-first argument,
for the reason recorded in §2.

## 1 · What the censuses settled

Four facts decide the shape of everything below, and each was verified
against the working tree rather than inherited from an earlier document.

**Nothing borrows the source bytes.** Every byte the tree keeps is copied
out of the fed buffer before the feed returns: the line into `curline`, the
remainder into the node's content strbuf, inline literals into a chunk that
borrows the *parent's content* rather than the input, marks and concrete
records as integer offsets. Today's code already proved it — the joined
buffer was freed the instant `document_build` returned. So a chain-owned
buffer may grow and move without invalidating any document already built.

**`refine_blocks` is terminal, and it is the only thing that is.** It stamps
the tree, then does `res = parser->root; parser->root = NULL`, and a parser
whose root is NULL ignores every further feed. Nothing else in the parse
path is one-shot. A warm tick therefore may not call it; the fallback still
can, because the fallback builds its own parser.

**`finalize` is the sole writer of end coordinates, and there is no
un-finalize.** It asserts the node is OPEN before clearing the flag, and
`finalize_blocks` closes the whole spine to the root and destroys the held
partial line. A living tree must be closed to be *published* and must be
reopened to keep *growing* — which is the one claim in this milestone whose
failure has no cheap answer.

**Inline literals borrow the block's content buffer.** A unit that has been
refined holds children pointing into `node->content.ptr`; `add_line` grows
that buffer. So a warm tick's first act on the open leaf must be to retire
its inline children (and free its `inline_concrete`, which is a once-only
assignment), and only then feed bytes. This is the one genuine
use-after-free the milestone creates, and it is not at the byte layer.

## 2 · The order, and why

The risk-first lens named the claim that, if false, forces a redesign back
toward the buried fork: *a tick can speculatively close the open spine to
publish a correct projection, and then exactly undo that close so the next
tick continues as if it never happened.* Everything else in L1 — ownership
moving to the chain, ids minted at creation, revisions by touch — is a
refactor whose failure mode is a bug. That claim is provable COLD: with no
living tree, no ownership change, and no edit to `markdown_core_document_append`.
So it goes early, and the enabling refactors follow it rather than precede
it.

## 3 · Hazards the slices must answer

Each was raised by a census with file:line evidence and is not yet closed;
the slice that owns it is named.

- **The tree dies with the receiver** (slice 5). The tree is allocated from a
  per-document arena and the contract lets a caller free a superseded handle
  at any moment — including the ES and Kotlin reclaim paths, which do
  exactly that. The arena moves to the chain in the same motion as the tree.
- ~~**Fallback after mutation**~~ — CLOSED in slice 7: the decision is a
  pure probe over the previous publish's record and the arriving bytes,
  taken before a retract, a feed or a write; a refusal costs exactly that
  read. And should a line the predicate admitted retype a spine block after
  all, the publish comes back `final` and the tick fails as an engine
  contradiction rather than reopening a heading as a paragraph.
- ~~**Stale hashes corrupt the next fallback**~~ — CLOSED in slice 7: a warm
  tick stamps every subtree the spine gained (before the frontier diff, which
  pairs on those hashes) and restamps each spine block deepest first; a
  settled node's subtree is immutable, so nothing else can have moved. (Moot
  since the funeral: there is no fallback, and a hash is stamped only on
  what the frontier pairs.)
- ~~**The half line is not always truncatable**~~ — CLOSED in slice 7: the
  predicate judges the held line's first byte and every byte of it, the
  paragraph's content start, and every line the chunk begins; the byte sets
  are named per construct in `warm_byte_opens`.
- ~~**Kotlin's reuse gate inverts**~~ — CLOSED in slice 4.5: the receiver's
  root extent is now read before the append, so the bound describes the
  receiver even once a successor can share its predecessor's root.
- **The local UBSan is blind to the whole zero-length-on-null class.**
  Apple's sanitizer reports neither a zero offset applied to a null pointer
  (slice 1's `buffer + len`) nor a null passed where a library declares its
  parameter non-null (slice 3b's `memcpy(dst, NULL, 0)`); the Linux
  toolchains CI runs report both, and both were caught there after a green
  local four-config run. Naming `pointer-overflow` explicitly does not help
  — measured: Apple clang accepts the flag and still says nothing. Empty
  buffers, empty chunks and empty counts must be reasoned about, not merely
  run: guard the length before the call, once, where the call lives.
- ~~**A hook nothing invokes**~~ — found by the 6.7 census and DELETED in
  the same breath. `markdown_core_prepare_inline_domain_func` had a typedef,
  a field in the extension descriptor, two implementations and two
  registrations, and no caller anywhere; its signature took a
  `committed_owner`, and the commit that last touched it is the one that
  deleted the incremental reuse machinery (#98). It was that machinery's
  last stranded reader.
- **The record does not cover `node->concrete`** (L2). Prose lines capture
  no block-level marker on a spine node, so today's record has nothing to
  restore there; the first spine shape that does (a block quote's `>` per
  line, a list item's marker) must save each spine node's marker count at
  publish and truncate at retract, or a retract-and-refeed duplicates the
  markers. Named by the adversarial review of slice 7.
- **The 39-entry inventory's coordinates have drifted.** L0 shifted
  `core/blocks.c`; the measured deltas are −8 for the line-processing sites,
  −6 for `finalize_blocks`, +1 for `finalize`/`add_child`. Verify every
  coordinate against the working tree before using it as a journal spec.

## 4 · The first-principles form, and what L1 is short of it

Written 2026-08-16 after L1 landed, in answer to the question whether the
warm tick is the fundamentally right architecture and not merely the
smallest one that met the bound.

**What is fundamental.** CommonMark's block grammar is line-oriented and
prefix-stable except in three named places: the OPEN CHAIN (which is, by
definition, still subject to the next line), a paragraph's RETYPE by the
line that follows it (setext underline, table delimiter row), and
DEFINITIONS, which change the meaning of `[label]` anywhere in the document
whenever they arrive. Everything else a closed block is, it stays. So a
parser that has consumed lines L₁…Lₖ holds a state Sₖ, and the document at
any byte boundary is Sₖ with the held partial line P closed as if the input
ended there: `Tree(bytes) = close(Sₖ, P)`. An append moves Sₖ forward by the
lines the chunk completes and changes P. The only impurity is that `close`
is destructive in the parser as written — it finalizes blocks, moves
buffers, harvests definitions, frees a definitions-only paragraph — and
Sₖ must survive it. Every alternative that keeps Sₖ untouched by building
`close` on a copy (the fork; a shadow closure; a reader-side stitched view)
founders on the same fact: the AST is an intrusive tree with one parent
pointer per node, so a copied tail cannot share the settled prefix without a
seam that mutates it, and the plan buried exactly that. Which leaves the one
form that is both correct and minimal: **`close` runs on Sₖ itself and is
UNDONE by a record of what it changed** — retract, feed, settle, publish.
That is the tick, and it is not a mechanism bolted onto the parser; it is
the parser's own line loop with the closure made reversible. Object
identity for what settles; a bounded pairing for the tail that is
re-derived from P every tick, since P is re-derived every tick.

**What is not fundamental in L1, and why it exists.** The record covers the
closure of a PROSE spine (document over one paragraph or heading) and
nothing else, and the eligibility predicate is the guard that keeps a close
inside it. Everything downstream of that partiality is scaffolding, not
architecture: the predicate itself (byte sets, a content-start rule, the
byte-order-mark cases), the `final` flag and the post-feed self-check
(defenses for a predicate miss), the terminal close (a build whose end state
the record cannot reopen), the rebuild (a full parse when the record cannot
follow), and with it the whole-tree diff and the whole-tree hash stamp that
only the rebuild pairs on. AGENTS.md names this shape — a mode flag, a
shape-based branch, defenses — as evidence that the model is short of the
requirement, and it is: the plan staged it deliberately (L1 prose, L2 total,
L3 the funeral), and the staging is what the milestone table above records.

**The correct form** is the record made TOTAL, at which point every one of
those pieces is deleted rather than justified:

1. **The feed is always real.** No probe over the chunk's bytes: a
   complete line the chunk brings is processed for good, exactly as a
   one-shot parse would, and needs no undoing. What must be judged is only
   the STATE the feed leaves — the open chain and the held line — and once
   every state's close is retractable, nothing is judged at all.
2. **Every close is retractable.** Per spine block the record holds what
   the 39-entry inventory says the close can touch: type, flags,
   coordinates, content size, the payload union (list tightness, code and
   HTML literals moved out of the content buffer — moved back at retract,
   the fence's dropped info line re-inserted from the saved bytes), the
   marker-record count, the sibling before it (definitions and split-off
   paragraphs are INSERTED before a spine block); per parser the scalars,
   marks, held line, diagnostics count and both definition tables' sizes
   (a table truncates back to its size, since entries append). A close
   that REPLACES a spine block (the formula promotions) hands the replaced
   node to the record instead of freeing it, so the retract swaps it back;
   a close that FREES one (a definitions-only paragraph) detaches it onto
   the record instead. Then the terminal close goes, the rebuild goes, and
   every build ends published.
3. **Definitions re-refine what mentioned them.** A settled unit keeps the
   labels its inline parse probed (hits and misses, as 64-bit hashes); a
   definition registering — by the feed for good, by the close
   tentatively — re-refines exactly the units that probed that label. That
   is the plan's probe/flip design; it is what makes a late definition
   cost the units that mention it rather than the document. Two things the
   build settled beyond it (E, below): the units are FOUND through an
   index the probes themselves form — a link per (unit, label), threaded
   by label, unthreaded in its own size when the unit's probes go — since
   a walk of the tree per pending label is the O(document) the flip was
   meant to avoid; and a tentative flip is undone NOT by putting the old
   children back but by refining the unit again at the end of the retract,
   against the tables as they are then (same bytes, same answer, new
   objects), with what the flip had published kept for the next tick's
   pairing — because the old children's ids were paired away when the flip
   published, and putting them back would either resurrect retired ids or
   hand the next flip a run to pair against that was never published.
4. **Identity is object identity plus one pairing.** Settled nodes are the
   same objects for the stream's life; the tail re-derived from P pairs
   against the tail it replaces by the diff's own plan (hash sweeps over the
   tail's subtrees, positional middle by type, residue minted, each pair
   classified by projection), so an unchanged inline node keeps its
   revision and an empty append moves nothing. The whole-tree diff dies;
   the plan and the machine survive at the frontier only, and so do the
   stamps, over the frontier's subtrees only.
5. **What stays a wall, honestly.** The open leaf is re-refined and
   re-paired every tick — O(leaf), which for a paragraph that never closes
   is the document (the ladder's prose wall); a fence's buffer moves twice
   per tick (memcpy speed); a definition storm costs the mentioning units.
   None of these is O(document) for the shapes real prose has, and none of
   them needs a second path.

**The order the remaining work takes**, each step deleting part of the
predicate as it lands and none of them adding a path: (A) — LANDED
2026-08-16 — containers and leaves whose close moves no bytes — block
quotes, lists and items, ATX and setext headings, thematic breaks — through
the payload snapshot, the marker-record count and the memo a list's
finalize leaves on its items, so the spine may be any depth of them; with
it the record grew the sibling before its youngest child (so what a close
INSERTS there is inside the run a step refines), `final` became an ordinary
outcome — the close of a spine block the record cannot put back (a fence, a
table, an extension's) publishes correctly and closes the build for good,
the next append rebuilds — the post-feed self-check went, and the predicate
shrank to what it is FOR: no line begins a definition (`[` after any
container markers, since a definition inside a quote or an item is one), no
line begins a standalone formula (`$`, `\`), and a leaf paragraph's content
starts with none of those — a held line that is only whitespace is a blank
line at the close, and what a blank line writes, onto the current block's
youngest child, the record now holds (`last_child_flags`); the stream case's
share went from 942 warm / 328 rebuilt to 1229 / 41, warm_close_undo's
cuts to 401 of 412 with 3 closed for good, warm_tick_stream to 276 ticks
over lists, quotes and headings. Measured on the nested-list bench shape at
token strides: the ticks that still rebuild are exactly the cuts where a
held `-` under an open paragraph is a setext underline — a retype, step
(C)'s — three per unit; (B) — LANDED — code and
HTML blocks — the buffer moved back and the info line re-inserted; (C) —
LANDED — the retypes — setext and table conversion undone by type, payload
and the split-off paragraph; (D) — LANDED — the replacements — the formula
promotions handed to the record; (E) — LANDED — definitions — table sizes
and the flip machinery, real and tentative; (F) — LANDED, in two
cuts — the funeral. First the predicate, the terminal close and the "prose
subset", with `final` kept as one honest fallback (a third-party extension
block whose payload is not described) and the ledger's `rebuilt_ticks`

Then, after #105 merged, the fallback itself: an extension that opens

The subtree hash survives as the FRONTIER's pairing key and nothing else:
stamped on exactly the subtrees a tick pairs (the run each spine block
gained, a flipped unit's new and old children) and read nowhere else, so the
positional fold, the carried prefix folds and their corrections went with
the whole-tree stamp, and a one-shot parse stamps nothing (~6–11% of its
time, back). Steps A–D were undo entries over the inventory's sites; E was
the one piece of new machinery; F was subtraction.

**What E and F found that the roadmap did not name.** Each of these was
a measured O(document) term or a defect the corpora surfaced, and each is
now in the tree with a killer:

- *The probe index* (map.h). One tree walk per pending label made a
  definition-dense stream O(document) per tick; the probes are now the
  index (above).
- *The positional fold* (node.h, between #105 and the funeral). A flip
  changed a settled unit's hash, and the sequential fold restamped every
  ancestor whole — the root is the document — and forced the record's
  carried prefix folds to be recomputed; the subtree hash became a SUM of
  index-weighted child hashes so that a settled child's change moved each
  ancestor by one product, and a footnote per two lines streamed at 1–2 µs
  a tick, flat. The funeral then made every-node freshness a non-goal (a
  hash is read only where the frontier pairs), and the fold went back to
  the plain mix; the per-tick figures held.
- *Tightness, weighed incrementally.* A list's finalize weighed every
  item at every close; an item with a sibling after it is settled and what
  it weighs never changes, so items are marked as weighed and a finalize
  weighs what grew (a tight list of 100k items: 63 µs a tick and growing,
  to 0.68 µs flat). With it, the memo of "ends with a blank line" keeps
  its ANSWER — cmark's returns a container's own flag on a second ask,
  which a one-shot parse never makes and a stream makes at every close —
  and every node the memo is set on is closed, so it stays; the retract
  arm that cleared it was deleted by mutant.
- *A map entry wins its label through the prepared index*, and a
  truncation removes in place (backward shift), so a one-shot parse with
  many definitions is linear again — the first form scanned the live chain
  and made `footnote_dense`'s one-shot parse quadratic.
- *Diagnostics belong to their refine.* A re-refined unit raised its
  inline diagnostics again; each now carries its unit, a flip drops (or,
  when the flip may be undone, hides) the unit's own, and the document
  sorts by position when it takes them.
- *A leaf the feed takes.* A definitions-only paragraph the FEED closes
  leaves for good, and the record that named it as its open leaf read it
  freed (ASan, the api test). The parser keeps what it takes on a list
  until the caller has looked; the identity step retires the leaf's
  frontier whole and pairs its parent's run — everything after the
  sibling it followed — against both of what the last close retired
  there.
- *A retired id came back* twice: the vanished leaf revived by the
  retract, and a unit's pre-flip children — both minted fresh now, since
  a retired id never returns.
- *The directive extension describes its payload* (and its restore frees
  the attributes JSON an accessor may have rendered onto a published
  projection); an extension that allocates no payload keeps its state in
  the union the record copies (a task item), so both are retractable —
  and after the funeral an extension that opens blocks and allocates
  payloads without describing them is not attached at all.
- *A blank line writes onto a settled node's youngest child* and *a
  spine block's carried prefix must stop at the sibling before its
  youngest child, not at the child* — the close may insert between them,
  or take the child out — were both found by the stamp-freshness oracle.

## 5 · What this branch does not do

No intra-unit incremental inline parsing, and no harvest prefix
memoization — the plan's non-goals — so the honest ladder keeps its
entries: a leaf that is the document (one paragraph, one growing fence, one
paragraph of consecutive definitions) costs the leaf per tick, and the
bench arms record it. Of the plan's L3 funeral, one thing stays by design:
the frontier's pairing machine (`diff.c`'s plan and run over two child
lists) and the subtree hash it sweeps on — because a tick re-derives the
tail of the tree from the held partial line, and the contract's "an empty
append moves no revision" needs that tail paired, not re-minted. Everything
else the plan staged for L2 and L3 — journal totality, the fallback rate at
zero and then no fallback at all, the amortized-bound gate, the whole-tree
diff and stamp deleted — has landed.
