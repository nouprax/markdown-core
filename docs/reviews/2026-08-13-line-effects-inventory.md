# The one-line side-effect inventory

Status: EVIDENCE, frozen. Companion to `2026-08-13-living-tree-plan.md` §3:
this is the enumeration that makes the one-line undo journal buildable, and
it becomes the journal's specification when milestone L2 starts. Produced by
the 2026-08-13 audit (21 agents over the post-#103 tree); every entry carries
the write site and its evidence lines.

Classification legend, matching the plan:

- **APPEND-SHAPED** — undo is truncate/pop.
- **REPLACING** — undo is restore-saved-value (subclasses: ephemeral,
  self-restoring, sticky, set-only, irreversible-without-saved-bytes).
- **STRUCTURAL** — undo needs an operation log entry.
