THE YAML PARSER'S READINGS (elements/yaml.h), frozen from the parser reworked
from libyaml's scanner and parser that the recursive-descent parser replaced:
the two read about six million generated texts, every text of up to five
characters over the structural alphabet among them, to the same events. Each
case after this header is a text on one line after "> ", with \\, \n, \r,
\t and \xHH escapes, and the events it reads to, one a line, or "error":
+STR -STR, +DOC and -DOC (" ---" or " ..." when a marker writes it), +MAP and
+SEQ (" {}" or " []" in flow), -MAP -SEQ, and =VAL with its bytes as written
(from-to), its line, its style (: plain, ' and " quoted, | literal) and its
text; then "#from-to" for each comment. A blank line ends a case. The cases are a
shape each: the shortest text for each sequence of events and styles the
generated texts read to, some longer ones, and for each problem a few texts
that have it.
