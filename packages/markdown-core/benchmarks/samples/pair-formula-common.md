The ratio `r = I_c / I_m` is reported per stage, and the aggregate
`R = \sum_i w_i x_i` is reported over the concatenated corpus.

Inline `x^2 + y^2 = z^2` and `\alpha\beta\gamma` appear beside ordinary
*emphasis* and **strong** runs, so the formula scanner shares each line
with the delimiter stack rather than running on a line of its own.

`A longer run of mathematics that spans a whole clause and closes late`
in the paragraph, and `n` closes immediately after one character.

Several `a` runs `b` on `c` a `d` single `e` line keep the scanner
opening and closing without any prose between them.

A standalone ``y`` sits inline beside `z` in the same clause.
