Inline notes ^[measured on one toolchain] carry their content where they
are written rather than through a definition, and a note may hold
*emphasis* ^[with **strong** content] or a [link](/u).

Several ^[one] in ^[two] one ^[three] paragraph ^[four] number themselves
by position, so the counter advances without any label to resolve.

A longer one ^[a note whose content runs past a clause and closes at the
end of it] wraps across the line it was opened on.
